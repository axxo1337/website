### Onto language

Why is it that we cannot describe anything with certainty? The answer is not surprising by any means, but digging into its implications 
might be.

For a lack of a better word, I call this property of language discrete. Something is discrete when it deals with things by counting them as 
seperate distinct pieces. 

The world itself, on the other hand, is continuous. It is a never-ending stream of properties which flows with time.
