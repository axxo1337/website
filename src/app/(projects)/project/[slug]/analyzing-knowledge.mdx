### Onto language

Why is it that we cannot describe anything with certainty? The answer is not surprising by any means, but digging into its implications 
might be.

For a lack of a better word, I call this property of language discrete. The property of being inexact or abstracting.
Something is said to be discrete when it deals with objects by counting them as seperate distinct pieces. 

The world itself, on the other hand, is continuous. It is a never-ending stream of properties which flow with what we perceive as time.
